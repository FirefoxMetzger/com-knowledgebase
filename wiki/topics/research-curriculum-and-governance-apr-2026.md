# Research, curriculum & org governance (Apr 2026 inbox)

**What this note is:** A **topic map** for **teaching, research communication, and organizational control** in **2026-04-14**–**2026-04-22**—**explainer** content (tokens), **architecture** and **world-model** discourses, **paper roundups**, **K–12 / intentionality** interviews, and **enterprise** governance of **multi-agent** systems. For **safety research** in the alignment sense, use [[ai-safety-alignment-and-model-eval-apr-2026]]; for **Palantir**-style comms, both this page and [[public-narrative-robotics-and-trust-apr-2026]] apply.

**Agent-oriented summary (themes):**

- **Tokens 101 (*Turing Post*):** A didactic issue explaining what a **token** is and how tokenization underpins model economics—suitable for **onboarding** questions; verify **vendor-specific** pricing against live docs.
- **Multi-agent governance (webinar / sponsor):** The Beehiiv issue on **governing** multi-agent systems at **scale** is largely a **sponsored** **Galileo** / **CrewAI** event promo—use it as a **vocabulary** pointer (policies, cost, compliance), not as a neutral survey of the field.
- **TheSequence “Knowledge / Opinion / AI of the week” line:** The inbox has installments on **world models** (#842), a **Mythos**-related **essay** (cross-ref [[ai-safety-alignment-and-model-eval-apr-2026]]), **Harness** and **agentic software engineering** opinion (#844), a **radar** digest (#845), **beyond transformers** (#846), and a deep **Opus 4.7** #847 (cross-ref [[claude-opus-4-7]]).
- **Academic / arXiv roundups (*NLP News* “top papers”)** and **3D / world model** *Neuron* pieces—suitable for “what the newsletter bubble surfaced” in **ML research**, not a substitute for reading papers.
- **Spec-driven practice (*DeepLearning.AI*):** Overlaps [[agentic-coding-and-tooling-apr-2026]]; here it matters as **pedagogy** (how to **teach** agent workflows).
- **Claude “Managed Agents” product review (*Creators' AI*):** A single issue-length **review** of Anthropic’s **managed agents** product—treat as **one author’s** experience.
- **Workplace and PM practice (*AI Product Playbook*):** A **guest** conversation about **PMs** who rarely touch **production** and a **YC**-shaped path—**culture** and **process**, not a statistical study of PM behavior.
- **Palantir manifesto virality (*Turing Post*):** Analytic piece on **comms** and **narrative**; pair with the governance theme when a question is “how enterprises talk about AI.”
- **Neeru Khosla / “intention” in education (*Turing Post*):** Long interview framing **pedagogy and intentionality** in an AI-heavy world; good for **values / curriculum** questions, weak for **edtech** product specs. For the **“classified frontier”** essay (secrecy / geopolitics of research), see raw link under [[public-narrative-robotics-and-trust-apr-2026]].
- **AI Tidbits (single older-style capture):** A **“ahead of the curve”** curation; lower overlap with the rest of the window but part of the **self-education** cluster.
