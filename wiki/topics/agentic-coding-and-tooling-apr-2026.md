**What this note is:** A **topic map** for how newsletters framed **AI-assisted software development** during **2026-04-14**–**2026-04-22**—from **spec- and plan-driven workflows** to **agent products in the browser and IDE** (Codex, Cursor, Claude Code, Chrome Skills) and the **narrative of an OpenAI “superapp”** inside developer tools. This is a digest of digest claims; treat metrics and release names as **unverified** until you read the linked raw files or the cited primary sources inside them.

**Agent-oriented summary (themes):**

- **Spec beats “vibe coding” (training / promo content):** DeepLearning.AI positioned **spec-driven development** with **coding agents** as a way to keep outputs aligned: project constitutions, plan–implement–verify loops, and reusable “agent skills” instead of ad hoc prompting alone.
- **Browser and OS integration:** Multiple issues tied **OpenAI** and **Google** product moves to **Chrome**-level skills or automation and to **Claude Code**-style local workflows, often in the same breath as **GPT-5.4-Cyber** and “mythos” / security subplots (see also [[ai-safety-alignment-and-model-eval-apr-2026]]).

- **“Superapp” inside Codex:** At least one publication summarized OpenAI as bundling a broader **app/surface** story around **Codex**; confirm wording in the raw export and any linked product posts.
- **End-user agents:** The same window includes **NLP News “AI Agents Weekly”** cross-links that mention **Opus 4.7** and **Codex** ubiquity; pair with model-specific note [[claude-opus-4-7]].
