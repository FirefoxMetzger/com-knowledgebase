**Agent-oriented summary (themes):**

- **Automated alignment researchers (AARs):** *Import AI* summarized Anthropic work where **parallel Claude-based agents** ran experiments in **weak-to-strong supervision**, reported **PGR**-style metrics, costs in the low tens of thousands of dollars, and caveats on **overfitting and limited transfer** to other stacks—useful for knowing **which benchmark family** the story referenced (weak-to-strong), not for quoting exact numbers from this note.
- **Internal lab dynamics:** *The Neuron* ran a piece framed as an Anthropic model **outperforming internal human researchers** on a narrow class of tasks—treat as **journalistic** until checked against the underlying study and definitions of “win.”
- **Open-weights and geopolitics of eval:** *Import AI* also summarized **independent** evaluation of a major **Kimi** release with emphasis on **refusal / CBRN / cyber** axes and a **fine-tuning strip-safety** example—cited as a **safety signpost** for frontier open models, with all the usual **eval brittleness** warnings.
- **Mythos and narrative as safety discourse:** *TheSequence* and other outlets in this window used **Mythos**-adjacent storylines; pair with the **Automated** alignment and **AAR** item above when a question conflates **fiction/brand** with **technical controls**.
