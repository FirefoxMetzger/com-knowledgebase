# AI newsletter inbox

**Idea:** The Inkbox identity **sally-anderson** collects **AI- and tech-oriented email newsletters** (Substack, Beehiiv, TLDR, DeepLearning.AI promos, etc.). Raw captures live under `raw/emails/newsletters/`; the compiled index is [[../sources/newsletters-catalog]].

**What is in the first capture window:** Through **2026-04-22** (per message `created_at`), the vault holds **56** newsletter Markdown exports. **Welcome-only**, **double opt-in**, and other onboarding-only messages are not kept in `raw/`. What remains is mostly **dated digest / issue** traffic from **2026-04-14** onward (The Neuron, TLDR, The Rundown, Superhuman, TheSequence, Exponential View, Turing Post, NLP News, and others), plus a few first-day files that still included **curated links** (e.g. AI Tidbits’ “Recent posts” block).

**Uncertainty:** Paid vs free tiers, confirmation links still pending, and true sending cadence are only authoritative in the mailbox UI; archived files are static snapshots with envelope metadata in each header.
