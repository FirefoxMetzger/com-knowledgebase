# AI newsletter inbox

**Idea:** The Inkbox identity collects a set of **AI- and tech-oriented email newsletters** (mostly Substack, plus Beehiiv and TLDR). The first batch of raw captures (2026-04-13) is almost entirely **welcome** and **subscription-confirmation** traffic, not regular digest issues.

**Uncertainty:** Cadence, paid vs free tiers, and which confirmations still need a click-through are only visible inside the mailbox or provider UI; the raw files are a static snapshot.
